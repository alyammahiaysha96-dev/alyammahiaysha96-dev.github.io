I

Chapter one · The idea

Every object has a secret it's dying to tell.

A dusty fan sighs. A liver panics over soda. A jar refuses to open. SceneLabs started in Dubai with one question: what if the things around us could speak? No presenter, no face on camera. The objects are the cast.

II

Chapter two · The scene

Then we build the world around it.

Lighting, set, character, voice. Every frame is written, voiced, animated and edited in-house with a modern AI production line, so the look stays premium and the pace stays social.

From the studio floor 01 / 08
III

Chapter three · The drama

A hook in the first second. A twist before the loop.

Our signature arc: hook, real problem, emotional beat, quick fix. A blender that dreads the sink, a lint screen gasping for air, a sponge that turns out to be alive. Small dramas in under a minute that people share and save.
