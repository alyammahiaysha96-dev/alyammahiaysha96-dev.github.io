Home Support

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy applies to children’s apps published by Aaesha Alyammahi, including Color Fun: Kids Coloring Book, Tiny Baby Phone, Animal Piano Friends, Cake Party Decorate, Pop Count Bubbles, Trace ABC & Arabic, Cute Animal Match for Kids, Shape Sorter Play, Hatch Dino Eggs, Kids Car Wash Garage, and Idris & Sara: Kids Duas.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. These apps do not have accounts, sign-in, chat, analytics of our own, or a way for a child to share personal details with us. Progress, language, and similar settings stay on the device.

Advertising

The apps may show banner ads from Google AdMob on menu or collection screens, never during coloring or play. Ads are configured as child-directed, limited to a G (general audience) content rating, and non-personalized. We do not use the ads to track users across apps or websites, and we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google may process limited technical information to deliver and measure those ads under its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s guidance for child-directed apps.

In-app purchases

Some apps offer an optional Remove Ads purchase. It is protected by a parental gate and is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy, email alyammahi.aysha96@gmail.com.
