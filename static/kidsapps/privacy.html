Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy applies to children’s apps published by Aaesha Alyammahi, including Color Fun: Kids Coloring Book.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. These apps do not have accounts, sign-in, chat, analytics of our own, or a way for a child to share personal details with us.

Advertising

The apps may show banner ads from Google AdMob on screens other than the coloring or play screen. Ads are configured as child-directed, limited to a G (general audience) content rating, and non-personalized. We do not use the ads to track users across apps or websites, and we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google may process limited technical information to deliver and measure those ads under its own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s guidance for child-directed apps.

Contact
